Overview

Mersir Automation is a private automation environment used for personal and internal workflows. It may connect to Google services, including Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Sheets, when an authorized user grants access.

Google data

Google account data is accessed only to perform the workflows explicitly authorized by the user. Depending on the workflow, this may include reading email messages, reading or writing spreadsheet data, and creating, reading, organizing, or storing files in Google Drive.

How data is used

Data is used only to execute the user's private automation workflows, maintain workflow records, and support troubleshooting or recovery when necessary. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Sharing

Data is not shared with unrelated third parties except where required to provide the underlying services used by the workflow, or where required by law. Access is limited to authorized users and service providers necessary to operate the automation.

Storage and security

Workflow data may be stored in the user's n8n environment, Google Drive, Google Sheets, or other systems selected by the authorized user. Reasonable measures are used to restrict access to authorized users.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for the user's workflows and recordkeeping. The authorized user may remove workflow data, revoke Google access, or request deletion by contacting the address below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
